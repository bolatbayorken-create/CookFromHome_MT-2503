Preparation

Revive Wilted Herbs

Soak limp cilantro, parsley, or lettuce in a bowl of ice water with a splash of lemon juice for 15–30 minutes to make them crisp again.

15–30 min
Seasoning

Fix Over-Salted Soup

Add unsalted broth or water a little at a time, then taste again. Increase the other ingredients if the soup becomes too thin.

Add a little, then taste
Ripening

Ripen Avocados Fast

Place an unripe avocado in a brown paper bag with a banana or an apple. Natural ethylene gas released by the fruit accelerates ripening.

A paper bag helps
Preparation

Prevent Onion Tears

Chill the onion in the refrigerator for 30 minutes before cutting. Lower temperatures slow down the release of the enzyme that causes crying.

30 min in the fridge
Storage

Test Egg Freshness

Submerge eggs in a bowl of cold water. If they sink flat to the bottom, they are very fresh. If they stand up or float, they are older. This does not confirm whether an egg is safe: check the date and storage conditions.

Check the date and storage
Baking

Soften Hard Brown Sugar

Place hardened brown sugar in a microwave-safe bowl with a damp paper towel and microwave in 20-second bursts until soft.

20-second bursts

Ingredient swaps

A few substitutions for everyday cooking.

Buttermilk

1 cup
Use instead

1 tbsp lemon juice + milk

Add milk to make 1 cup total. Rest for 5 minutes.

Sour cream

1 cup
Use instead

1 cup plain Greek yogurt

A similar texture and tang for everyday cooking.

Brown sugar

1 cup
Use instead

1 cup white sugar + 1 tbsp molasses

Mix together before adding to your batter.

Egg in baking

1 egg
Use instead

¼ cup unsweetened applesauce

For moisture in cakes and muffins.
